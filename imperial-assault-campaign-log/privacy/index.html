Privacy Policy for Imperial Assault Campaign Log

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Imperial Assault Campaign Log is an unofficial companion app for the Star Wars: Imperial Assault board game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores and how it is handled.

1. Summary

Imperial Assault Campaign Log does not collect your personal data and does not send it to the developer or to third parties. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting services.

2. Information stored on your device

The app keeps a record of your campaign so you can continue playing between sessions. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • campaign settings, such as the set of game boxes you play with;
  • hero names, XP, credits and influence;
  • mission results and rewards;
  • purchases, sales and item transfers;
  • the history of changes made to the campaign.

This information is stored locally on your device. In the Android app it is stored in the app's private storage. In the web version it is stored in your browser's local storage. The developer does not operate a server that receives this data.

3. Information we do not collect

Imperial Assault Campaign Log does not collect or request:

  • your name, email address, phone number or other account information;
  • your location;
  • contacts, photos, camera or microphone access;
  • advertising identifiers or device identifiers.

The Android app does not request the Internet permission and does not request any other special permissions.

4. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party data-collection services, and does not share information with third parties.

The web version is served as static files by its hosting provider. The hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests those files, according to its own privacy practices. That information is not available to the app or used by the developer.

5. Backup files

The app can export a campaign to a backup file and restore it from a file. A backup is created or selected only as a result of an action initiated by you. The app does not upload the file anywhere.

If you copy, share or store a backup using another application or cloud storage provider, the handling of that file is governed by the privacy practices of the service you choose.

6. Data retention and deletion

Campaign information remains on your device until you:

  • delete the campaign or its records in the app;
  • clear the app's data in Android settings or your browser's site data; or
  • uninstall the Android app, after which Android removes its local data.

Because the developer does not receive your data, there is nothing stored with the developer to delete. Backup files that you created yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.

7. Children's privacy

Imperial Assault Campaign Log is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app changes, for example if a feature that handles information differently is added. The updated version will be published at this address, and the date at the top of the document shows when it was last updated.

9. Not an official product

Imperial Assault Campaign Log is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Lucasfilm, Disney, Fantasy Flight Games or Asmodee. Star Wars and Imperial Assault are trademarks of their respective owners.
