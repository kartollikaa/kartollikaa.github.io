Imperial Assault Campaign Log

Version 1.0.0 · Android 7.0 or newer

An unofficial campaign log for the Star Wars: Imperial Assault board game, Core Set campaign. Keep the campaign at the table and let the app do the counting: missions, rewards, XP, credits, the shop and side-mission draws. Works offline, in Russian and English.

Install

  • Open the downloaded file on your phone and allow installing apps from this source when Android asks.
  • A newer APK from this page installs over this one and keeps your campaigns.
  • The Google Play version is signed with a different key, so the two don't update each other. To switch, export a backup in the app, uninstall it, install the other version and restore the backup.

What's in 1.0.0

  • Create a Core Set campaign, pick heroes and the Empire class.
  • Record mission results, rewards and side-mission draws; every number comes from the campaign journal and the last action can be undone.
  • Shop: buy and sell items with a budget check; every item has an owner.
  • Back up a campaign to a file and restore it.
  • Long-press a campaign in the list to delete it.
  • Saves from other versions always open.

Privacy

The app sends crash reports and basic usage events through Firebase; campaign contents stay on your device. Details are in the privacy policy.
