Privacy Policy for Imperial Assault Campaign Log

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Imperial Assault Campaign Log is an unofficial companion app for the Star Wars: Imperial Assault board game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores, what technical information it may send to third-party services, and how that information is handled.

1. Summary

Your campaign stays on your device. The app has no user accounts and no advertising, and the developer does not operate a server that receives your campaign. The Android app may use Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config to understand how the app is used, to find crashes and to configure features. These services receive usage and technical information, never the contents of your campaign.

2. Information stored on your device

The app keeps a record of your campaign so you can continue playing between sessions. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • campaign names and settings, such as the set of game boxes you play with;
  • hero names, XP, credits and influence;
  • mission results and rewards;
  • purchases, sales and item transfers;
  • the history of changes made to the campaign.

This information is stored locally on your device. In the Android app it is stored in the app's private storage. In the web version it is stored in your browser's local storage. Campaign information is not sent to the developer, to Firebase or to other third parties.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's local data in your device backup and restore it when you reinstall the app. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

3. Information we do not collect

Imperial Assault Campaign Log does not collect or request:

  • your name, email address, phone number or other account information;
  • your precise location, such as GPS or device location;
  • contacts, photos, camera or microphone access;
  • advertising identifiers.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or other special permissions. Network access in the Android app is used only by the Firebase services described in this Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics

The Android app may use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features are used and to improve the app. Analytics events created by the app describe general actions, for example:

  • opening a screen;
  • creating, finishing or deleting a campaign;
  • recording a mission result;
  • recording a purchase, a sale or an item transfer;
  • undoing or correcting a record;
  • exporting or restoring a backup.

These events contain predefined categories, counts and application actions. They do not intentionally contain campaign or hero names, any text you type, the contents of your campaign or backup files, your name, email address or phone number.

Google Analytics for Firebase may also automatically collect technical and usage information needed to provide the service, such as app interactions, session information, app instance identifiers, the app version, device and operating system information, and approximate geographic information, such as country or region, derived from network information.

5. Crash reporting and diagnostics

The Android app may use Firebase Crashlytics to find crashes and technical problems. When the app crashes or meets certain errors, Crashlytics may receive diagnostic information such as:

  • crash stack traces;
  • the state of the app at the time of the crash;
  • device model, operating system version and app version;
  • installation identifiers;
  • other technical information related to the crash.

The app does not intentionally include campaign contents, hero or campaign names, or text you type in crash reports.

6. Firebase Remote Config

The Android app may use Firebase Remote Config to adjust or gradually turn on features without installing a new version. To do this, Remote Config may process technical information such as the app identifier and version, the operating system and its version, language, time zone, country code and the Firebase installation identifier. Remote Config is not used to upload your campaign.

7. Firebase, Google and the web version

Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config are provided by Google LLC. Information sent to these services is processed by Google on the developer's behalf to provide them, and is governed by Google's privacy and data-processing terms.

Firebase Privacy and Security

The web version does not include analytics, crash reporting or Remote Config. It is served as static files by its hosting provider. The hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests those files, according to its own privacy practices. That information is not available to the app or used by the developer.

8. How information is used and shared

Information received through Firebase services is used to:

  • understand how the app and its features are used;
  • find and fix crashes and other technical problems;
  • improve the app's stability, performance and usability;
  • configure or gradually introduce features.

The developer does not sell information, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with third parties other than Google as the provider of the services described above.

9. Backup files

The app can export a campaign to a backup file and restore it from a file. A backup is created or selected only as a result of an action initiated by you. The app does not upload the file anywhere, and the contents of backup files are not sent to Firebase.

If you copy, share or store a backup using another application or cloud storage provider, the handling of that file is governed by the privacy practices of the service you choose.

10. Data security

Information sent to Firebase services is protected in transit with encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Information stored on your device is protected by the security of your device, its operating system or your browser.

11. Data retention and deletion

Campaign information remains on your device until you:

  • delete the campaign or its records in the app;
  • clear the app's data in Android settings or your browser's site data; or
  • uninstall the Android app, after which Android removes its local data.

Analytics and diagnostic information sent to Firebase is kept for a limited time according to the retention settings and policies of those Google services. Uninstalling the app does not immediately delete information that was already sent. You can ask the developer to delete it by writing to the contact address below.

Backup files that you created yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.

12. Children's privacy

Imperial Assault Campaign Log is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app changes, for example if a feature that handles information differently is added. The updated version will be published at this address, and the date at the top of the document shows when it was last updated.
